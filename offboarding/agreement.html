Psychologist Offboarding & Client Transition

Please review this agreement carefully. By signing, you confirm your obligations regarding client handover, confidentiality, client data, and non-solicitation.

Psychologist Information

Agreement

1. Client Handover

I agree to cooperate in the orderly transition of all active Bicarakan.id clients to another Bicarakan.id psychologist designated by Bicarakan.id. I will complete required clinical notes and provide reasonable information necessary for continuity of care, including relevant clinical or safety concerns.

2. No Solicitation or Diversion

During my engagement and for 6 months after my final working date, I will not persuade, encourage, solicit, or arrange private services with an active Bicarakan.id client for the purpose of diverting that client from Bicarakan.id, or use Bicarakan.id client information to obtain clients for my own practice or another business. This does not restrict a client's independent choice of provider.

3. Confidentiality & Client Data

I will keep Bicarakan.id client information confidential and will not copy, export, download, retain, or use client records or contact information for personal or commercial purposes without proper authorization. These obligations continue after my engagement ends and remain subject to applicable Indonesian law and professional obligations.

4. Company Information & Systems

Upon termination, I will return Bicarakan.id property and cease unauthorized access to Bicarakan.id systems, records, accounts, and confidential information.

5. Breach & Acknowledgment

I understand that unauthorized use of client information, intentional client solicitation or diversion, or failure to complete the agreed handover may constitute a breach of my contractual obligations and may result in remedies available under applicable law. I confirm that I have read, understood, and agree to this Agreement.
